# BisaRx
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
Patients seeking symptom intake and pharmacist guidance; pharmacists reviewing cases and sending feedback; administrators managing staff and users.
## Product Purpose
Connect conversational symptom intake with human pharmacist review and patient feedback.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve existing API contracts, Ghana SMS and international email OTP, photo and voice input, multilingual support, body map, profile, case history, medication quotes, and delivery/payment workflows. Keep all records and permissions server-controlled. Never fabricate clinical activity or credentials.
## Brand Commitments
BisaRx name retained. User requested a complete visual replacement across every page: premium professional ChatGPT-style UI, modern icons and typography, clean animation. Light default with dark mode confirmed.
The subsequent refinement explicitly requests glassmorphism and purposeful modern motion, simpler menus, and removal of duplicate controls. Admin and Pharmacist access links must not appear on the patient page.
Retain the original supplied blue-and-green logo in static/logo.png across visible branding and the installed app icon.
## Operating Context
Existing static HTML/CSS/JavaScript frontend, FastAPI backend, Vercel frontend and Render backend. Code-led implementation for working app screens.
## Product Principles
Conversation first; clear human review; honest loading/error/empty states; accessible desktop and mobile navigation.
